01 · The person behind the pixels

Good technology should feel human.

I'm Abigiya Tigistu, a Software Engineering student at Jimma University, expected to graduate in 2028.

My focus is frontend development: responsive web experiences, intuitive interfaces, and digital products that make sense to the people using them. I enjoy the detail work—how a page reads, how it adapts, and how small decisions add up to a more thoughtful whole.

Studying in Jimma keeps my work connected to a real place and real needs. I'm building my skills through projects, practice, and the habit of staying curious.
